THE SETTING-UP

THE COMMANDS

command.run · sddSets this chat's item and opens the pane the first time.
sdd-view · sdd-worktrees · sdd-bugsOpen the view, the Worktrees page and the bug manager.
prompt.autocompleteSuggests open items after /sdd.

THE TOOL CALLS

tool.call · Bash | PowerShellAfter each sdd script run, follows the item it names and refreshes.
tool.call · AskUserQuestionShows the question in the stage's feedback box, where it can also be answered.

THE RECKONING

turn.start · turn.completeCharges each turn's tokens and dollars to its stage, and notifies once when the item waits on you.
session.measure · session.endTracks context fill and cost, and resets on /clear or /resume.

THE PAINTING

ui.render · SpinnerAdds the item, stage and goal after the spinner word.
ui.render · AbovePromptDraws the status band above the prompt.
ui.render · PaneDraws the sdd view, Worktrees and Bugs panes.
ui.closeNotes which pane closed.
