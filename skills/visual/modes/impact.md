# Mode: impact — present a blast-radius analysis

Used at: sdd:impact Step 5 (one item) and Step E (`impact all`). Source: `impact.md` /
`impact.json`. Default renderer: quick (full for `impact all` with many pairs).

Sections — use only those with something the user needs:

1. **Summary** — counts of Direct / Indirect / gap questions (first viewport).
2. **Map** — flow: the item in the middle, Direct items linked by labelled edges (`same file`,
   `shared term`, `parent`, `similar`), conflicts in `danger` tone.
3. **Direct** — table: item (ADO link), why it is affected, evidence.
4. **Gap questions** — numbered.
5. **Not checked** — what was out of reach (unsynced items) and why.
